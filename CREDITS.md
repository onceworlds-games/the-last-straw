# Credits

All pictures and animation are drawn by the game's own code (Canvas 2D); no image or model files are used. Music and
most voices (the camel, the cat, bells, rings) are synthesized with Web Audio. Recorded landings, footfalls, creaks,
coins and clicks come from Kenney's CC0 packs, trimmed and joined into one sprite (`game/sfx/sprite.mp3`).
