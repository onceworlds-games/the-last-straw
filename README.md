# The Last Straw

Take turns loading the camel. Don't be the one who breaks its back.

Play: https://onceworlds.com/play/the-last-straw

Pick one of two items from the cart (the other goes to the next player), set it on the load, and score its kg if the camel
holds; set it down soft for a bonus. Break its back, or drop something off, and you score nothing that round. Alone:
Caravan, a push-your-luck run from town to town (load it to the brink, set off, pick a gift at every town), and the Daily
cart. Levels, trade ranks from Peddler to Sultan, a Stable of coats, blankets, hats and sleeves to earn, and three goals
a day.

Plain ES modules and Canvas 2D in `game/`, no build. Physics by [planck.js](https://piqnt.com/planck.js/) (MIT, vendored in
`game/vendor`). How it plays and why: `DESIGN.md`.

Tests (Node 22 or newer, nothing to install): `npm test`.
